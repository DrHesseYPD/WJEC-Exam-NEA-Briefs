Task Requirements

  1. Part (a) [5 Marks]: Cleanse and organise the quantitative dataset using suitable spreadsheet formulas, functions, and skills (basic, medium, and complex).

Required Evidence

  • Cleansed and organised spreadsheet document.
  • Annotated screenshots or screen recording explaining applied spreadsheet techniques.
  • A structured findings report with visual graphs/charts.

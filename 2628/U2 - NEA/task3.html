Task Requirements

  1. Initial Design Options [10 Marks]: Plan annotated initial design options for:
    • A 3-page multipage website (minimum 2 initial design options).
    • A computer game (minimum 2 initial design options).
    • Annotations must communicate visual elements, sound aspects, client brief requirements, and qualitative data choices.
  2. Design Selection & Justification [5 Marks]: Select one chosen design option for each product and justify your selection against your Task 2 success criteria.

Required Evidence

  • Two initial design options for the website.
  • Two initial design options for the computer game.
  • Written justification document for your selected designs.

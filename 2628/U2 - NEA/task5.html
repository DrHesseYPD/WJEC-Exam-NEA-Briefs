Task 5: Testing & Evaluation (10 Marks)

Suggested Timing: 1 hour | Control Level: High

Task Requirements

  1. Part (a) - Testing Evaluation [5 Marks]: Evaluate the effectiveness of your ongoing testing approach. Explain how testing helped:
    • Identify and remove technical errors.
    • Ensure products operated as expected.
    • Verify that all success criteria were fully met.
  2. Part (b) - Audience Evaluation [5 Marks]: Evaluate how well your completed digital products meet your target audience's needs, trends, and preferences. Refer directly to:
    • The finished digital products (website and computer game).
    • Your overall development process.
    • Your application of technical skills.

Required Evidence

  • A written evaluation document (No raw screenshot testing logs required).

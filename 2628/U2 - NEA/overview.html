Assessment Overview & Timings

Unit 2 contributes 40% of the overall GCSE qualification grade and is marked out of 80 total marks over 20 total assessment hours under supervised conditions.

Recommended Time Allocation (20 Hours Total)

  • Task 1 (Data Analytics): ~2.5 hours
  • Task 2 (Proposal & Planning): ~1 hour
  • Task 3 (Design Options & Justification): ~2.5 hours
  • Task 4 (Development - Website & Computer Game): ~13 hours
  • Task 5 (Testing & Evaluation): ~1 hour

Key Candidate Rules

  • Digital Asset Collection: You are permitted to collect digital assets outside of official assessment hours.
  • Independent Work: Group work and collaboration are strictly prohibited.
  • Controlled Conditions: All tasks must be completed under high-control supervision with work securely stored between sessions.

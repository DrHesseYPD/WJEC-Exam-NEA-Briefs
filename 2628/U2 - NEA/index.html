Client Brief: Llynnoedd Cymru

Organisation Overview

Llynnoedd Cymru is an organisation committed to promoting physical activity, water safety, and community wellbeing across Wales. The organisation runs campaigns and training sessions designed to improve swimming participation and raise awareness of water safety.

Llynnoedd Cymru has identified that swimming participation levels, water safety confidence, access to lessons, and seasonal engagement vary significantly across Welsh local authorities. To respond to these challenges, the organisation is seeking a suite of digital products that will educate, inform, and engage communities across Wales.

Digital Product Development Requirements

  • Data Analytics: Initial research data on swim participation, ability, knowledge of water safety, and possible barriers must be cleansed and organised (LlynnoeddCymru_dataset_e.csv).
  • Design Alignment: Qualitative data in the form of a mood board (Mood_Board_e.png) is provided to convey initial design options.
  • Website Development: A multi-page website using provided text content (Webpage_e.txt).
  • Computer Game: A digital product aligning with data analytics outcomes.
  • Time Planning: A project schedule planned over a contextual 2-month development window.
Candidate Reminder: All work must be completed under controlled conditions independently. AI-generated content is strictly prohibited as your own work.

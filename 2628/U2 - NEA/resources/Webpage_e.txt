Page Title: 
Welcome to Llynnoedd Cymru: Dive Into Our Community

Page Text: 
Welcome to Llynnoedd Cymru! We are a passionate social enterprise dedicated to promoting physical activity, community wellbeing, and water safety across every local authority in Wales. Our mission is simple: to ensure that everyone, regardless of their background or location, has the opportunity to enjoy the physical and mental health benefits of swimming.

Across Wales, we know that access to aquatic facilities and swimming participation levels can vary significantly. Whether you are splashing around for leisure, swimming for fitness, or taking part in competitive training, we believe that water should be a safe and welcoming environment for all.

Through our community workshops, targeted campaigns, and expert training sessions, we are working hard to reduce inequalities in access. We are here to support you in finding local pools, breaking down barriers like transport and cost, and building a lifelong love for the water. Join us as we make a splash for a healthier, safer Wales!

Page Title: 
Water Safety and Building Confidence

Page Text: 
At Llynnoedd Cymru, water safety is at the heart of everything we do. Wales is home to beautiful coastlines, rivers, and lakes, but open water can be unpredictable. Did you know that confidence and water-safety knowledge vary greatly depending on whether you had access to swimming lessons as a child or through school provision?

We run public-facing initiatives and training sessions designed to boost your open-water confidence and teach essential survival skills. Our water-safety campaigns focus on educating the public on how to navigate hazards, understand tides and currents, and look out for one another in the water.

Whether you have never attended a swimming lesson or you're a seasoned club member looking to transition to open water, our educational resources are here to help. We advocate for stronger school swim provisions and provide community workshops to ensure that every generation has the vital knowledge they need to stay safe while enjoying our beautiful Welsh waters.

Page Title: 
Overcoming Barriers and Getting Involved

Page Text: 
We understand that getting into a pool isn't always easy. Our research shows that communities across Wales face different barriers to swimming - from the cost per session and lack of local facilities, to transport issues and a lack of personal confidence.

Llynnoedd Cymru is actively working to dismantle these barriers. We partner with local authorities to highlight where support is needed most, advocating for affordable swimming options and better transport links to local leisure centres. We also monitor seasonal engagement, knowing that visits can dip during the colder months, and we create winter initiatives to keep you active all year round.

Getting involved is easy! Whether you are looking to join a local swimming club, find beginner lessons for adults or children, or simply want to participate in our next community awareness campaign, there is a place for you here. Together, we can ensure that every community in Wales has fair access to the water. Check out our upcoming training sessions and events to start your swimming journey today.
